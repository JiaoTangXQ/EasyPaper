## Style Prompt

Light product explainer for EasyPaper. The video should feel like a clear walkthrough for people who have never used an AI paper-reading website. Use restrained interface mockups, side-by-side workflow contrast, and motion that explains fewer manual steps. Do not use exaggerated claims, cinematic effects, or decorative clutter.

## Colors

- Background: `#F5F7FB`
- Foreground text: `#111827`
- Secondary text: `#6B7280`
- Primary action blue: `#2563EB`
- Core conclusion highlight: `#FDE68A`
- Method highlight: `#BFDBFE`
- Data highlight: `#BBF7D0`
- Knowledge base purple: `#7C3AED`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#DDE3EE`

## Typography

- Primary: `"Helvetica Neue", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Use 700 weight for scene headlines, 400-500 for body and UI labels.

## Motion Rules

- Use 0.35-0.7s entrances with `power2.out` or `power3.out`.
- Use wipe overlays between scenes; do not fade scene content out before transitions.
- Animate workflow reductions through movement: scattered manual steps collapse into a structured EasyPaper flow.
- Keep captions stable at the bottom with scene-level timing.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not claim AI output is always correct.
- Do not show fake accuracy comparisons between PDF software and EasyPaper.
- Do not use dark sci-fi, neon, gradient-heavy, or marketing hero styling.
- Do not show paper content or highlights without a visible "示意" label.
- Do not make text-heavy frames that require pausing to read.
